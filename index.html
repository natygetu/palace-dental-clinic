Daily Care Tips

Brush twice a day

Brush morning and night for two minutes with fluoride toothpaste.

Clean between your teeth

Floss or use interdental brushes daily to remove what a toothbrush can't reach.

Go easy on sugary drinks

Sugar feeds the bacteria that cause cavities, so keep sweets and sodas occasional.

Visit your dentist regularly

Regular check-ups help find small problems before they become big ones.
